Thirty-two mazes of rock, one per screen, with the exit at the top and a minute or more on the clock: the hero breaks the blocks with blows, they fall with whatever sits on them, he lays his own to climb and dodges the ones that walk, roll and fly; every fourth stage is a BONUS GAME. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the five tunes, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump f924d89a...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM (checked against openMSX) and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, the blows and the blocks, the time, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the 32 mazes, the BONUS GAME, what can be picked up, the enemies and the ending with three moai.
  • The sprites: the hero and the ten kinds of things on the map put together from the ROM tables, frame by frame.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: the stage select, SELECT and ESC on the title, STOP to look around, a score that misses a text, unused texts.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the main loop, the map, the RAM and the tools.
